I'm building Walyverse, a French Minecraft universe, and I make nearly every piece of it myself.

  • The gameplay: 35+ custom Java plugins & systems (Paper/Folia, MySQL, Redis)
  • The network: a fleet of servers behind one Velocity proxy, scaling on its own
  • The infrastructure: Ferry, my own deployment platform
  • The web: Ferry's dashboard, a hand-made Azuriom theme, an in-game Stripe store

The banner is drawn by my own script. No generators, no templates. Contribution stats are GitHub's own, right below this README.
